# Code of conduct

tokensaver is a small project that welcomes everyone who wants to help, whatever their
experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most mistakes are misunderstandings.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Help newcomers: a first contribution is worth extra patience.
- Respect "no" from maintainers and contributors, and accept decisions gracefully.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Any behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

Report problems privately to **info@axieo.ca**. Reports are kept confidential. The
maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block anyone who breaks this
code, in any project space (issues, discussions, pull requests).
